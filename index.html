Department of Computer Science & Engineering • Theory Course: Cyber Security
Course Code
VA302 / VA402 / BCC301
L - T - P Structure
2 - 0 - 0 (Credits: 2)
Total Teaching Hours
40 Lecture Hours
Target Branches
CSE / CS-AI / AIML / DS / IT
Course Objectives: This course introduces students to fundamental concepts of cyber security, cyber threats, and cyber crimes. The course aims to build awareness about common cyber attacks, security technologies, and safe computing practices. Students will learn the basics of digital forensics and cyber laws in India along with organizational security policies.
🎯 Course Outcomes (COs)
  • CO1 [L1: Remember]: Define facts, terms and basic concepts of cyber security, cyber threats, cyber crimes and information security principles including CIA triad.
  • CO2 [L2: Understand]: Express concepts of cyber attacks, malware, social engineering techniques, and security challenges in mobile and wireless devices.
  • CO3 [L3: Apply]: Employ knowledge of tools and techniques used in cybercrime such as phishing detection, steganography, VPN, TLS/SSL, and basic network security mechanisms.
  • CO4 [L4: Analyze]: Analyze digital forensic investigation procedures, cyber security threats, and legal aspects of cyber security to identify vulnerabilities and security risks.

Unit 1 10 Hours
Introduction to Cyber Security
Cyber Security- Definition and Origins of the word Cybercrime and Information Security, Classifications of Cybersecurity, CIA, A Global Perspective on Cybercrimes, Cybercrime Era: Survival Mantra for the Netizens. Cyber offences: How Criminals Plan the Attacks, Malware and Ransomware, Social Engineering, Cyber stalking, DNS spoofing, phishing/vishing.
Unit 2 06 Hours
Cyber Crime for Mobile Devices
Mobile and Wireless Devices-Introduction, Proliferation of Mobile and Wireless Devices, Trends in Mobility, Credit Card Frauds in Mobile and Wireless Computing Era, Security Challenges Posed by Mobile Devices, Registry Settings for Mobile Devices, Authentication Service Security, Attacks on Mobile/Cell Phones, Mobile Devices: Security Implications for organizations, Organizational Measures for Handling Mobile, Organizational Security Policies and Measures in Mobile Computing Era.
Unit 3 08 Hours
Tools and Methods Used in Cybercrime
Introduction, Proxy Servers and Anonymisers, TLS/SSL, VPN, Steganography, DoS and DDoS Attacks, SQL Injection, Buffer Overflow, Attacks on Wireless Networks. OSINT: Introduction and Tools.
Unit 4 08 Hours
Computer Forensics
Introduction, Digital Forensics Science, The Need for Computer Forensics, Cyber Forensics and Digital Evidence, Forensics Analysis of E-Mail (Case Study), Digital Forensics Life Cycle, Chain of Custody Concept, Network Forensics, Approaching a Computer Forensics Investigation. Forensics and Social Networking Sites: The Security/Privacy Threats, Challenges in Computer Forensics.
Unit 5 08 Hours
Security Policies and Cyber Laws
Need for An Information Security Policy, Introduction to Indian Cyber Law, Objective and Scope of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023/2025, Intellectual Property Issues, Overview of Intellectual Property Related Legislation in India, Patent, Copyright, Trademarks, NIST.
